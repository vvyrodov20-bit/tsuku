Изображения созданы встроенным ImageGen. Исходные файлы пользователя сохранены; новые иллюстрации лежат рядом отдельными файлами. Все изображения включены в архив и не требуют внешних загрузок.

## Запросы ImageGen

### Falling Pickaxe
Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: the provided Falling Pickaxe artwork. Create a clean high-fidelity transparent PNG cutout of the central logo with pixel pickaxe and small flying debris. Preserve the recognizable exact lettering FALLING PICKAXE and pixel illustration, proportions, diamond turquoise pickaxe, textures. Remove only the flat blue background entirely. Keep central subject tightly framed with around 4% transparent padding, square image. No new objects, no panel, no background color, no checkerboard, no outer rectangle or extra words. Intended for a dark indigo website game-card artwork which protrudes outside the card. Actual alpha transparency.

### Tower Rush
Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: the supplied TOWER RUSH game artwork. Extract the large central TOWER RUSH logo alone, including the thick grey outer border, black backing, exact yellow TOWER and blue RUSH letters and their current irregular chunky shapes. Remove the sky, buildings, crane, ground, chest, all scenery. Keep recognizable logo exactly as reference. Center and fill about 90% of image width with logo. Transparent PNG actual alpha around outer silhouette, no background color or checkerboard, no added text, no new scene. This is a cutout for a dark indigo website card where the logo overhangs card edges.

### Bonus artwork
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent artwork for an existing dark navy indigo gaming website bonus banner. Create an original polished 3D cluster: an open midnight-violet gift cube with luminous icy cyan interior, satin lilac ribbon, three floating gold coin discs, a small translucent turquoise faceted crystal and two violet poker chips orbiting around it. Elegant premium game interface illustration, not childish. Isometric three-quarter view. Dramatic soft violet rim lighting, luminous cyan inner glow and warm gold highlights. Strong legible silhouette, tightly centered square composition, 7% transparent margin, actual alpha transparency, subtle contact shadow only. No people, no logo, no text, no numbers, no percentages, no background rectangle, no checkerboard. Cohesive with blue/cyan/violet cards and dark #0d1129 background.
